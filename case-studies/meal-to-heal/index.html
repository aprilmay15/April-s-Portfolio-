Case Study

Meal to Heal
for a better
tomorrow.

Meal to Heal is a community-centered brand concept that brings together nutritious food access, practical nutrition education, and shared experiences to help families build healthier habits and stronger connections.

01 / The Opportunity

More than
a box of food.

While many meal programs focus on addressing immediate food insecurity, Meal to Heal was developed as a community-centered brand concept designed to support longer-term well-being. By combining access to nutritious meals with practical nutrition education, family-friendly resources, and tools for building healthier routines, the concept aims to empower families with the knowledge and confidence to make informed food choices beyond a single meal.

“Fresh Food.
Stronger Communities”

02 / The Visual Identity

A brand rooted in nourishment

Warmth you can
feel.

Rounded lettering and a simple leaf motif make the identity feel friendly and approachable. Creams speak to growth and nourishment, coral and gold bring energy and human warmth.

An uplifting palette designed for the program's brand world.

03 / Experience Strategy

One mission.
Many touchpoints.

Meal to Heal's brand experience was designed to create a consistent, meaningful connection with families across digital and community touchpoints. From educational nutrition content and community events to strategic partnerships and outreach initiatives, each interaction was envisioned to reinforce the brand's mission, build consumer trust, and encourage long-term engagement. This integrated approach positions Meal to Heal as more than a food-access initiative—it establishes a recognizable, purpose-driven brand centered on education, empowerment, and community well-being.
