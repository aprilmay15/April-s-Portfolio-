Case Study

Meal to Heal
for a better
tomorrow.

A community-centered brand concept connecting fresh food access with nutrition education, shared experiences, and stronger families.

Explore the Case Study ↓

Independent Concept

ProjectMeal to Heal
DisciplineBrand Identity & Campaign Strategy
Creative DirectionApril Amedu
Project StatusSelf-Initiated Conceptual

01 / The Opportunity

More than
a box of food.

The existing behind-the-brand meal programs often focus only on immediate access. Meal to Heal was created as an independent concept that also gives families tools, nutrition education, and confidence to create healthier routines.

“Fresh Food.
Stronger Communities”

02 / The Visual Identity

A brand rooted in nourishment

Warmth you can
feel.

Rounded lettering and a simple leaf motif make the identity feel friendly and approachable. Creams speak to growth and nourishment, coral and gold bring energy and human warmth.

An uplifting palette designed for the program's brand world.

03 / Experience Strategy

One mission.
Many touchpoints.

The brand experience was designed to feel cohesive across both digital and community-facing moments—from nutrition content and events to partnerships and outreach.

01Website

Mission, impact, and social resources.

02Social

Food boxes, practical recipes, and local spotlights.

03Community workshops and nutrition education
04Volunteer opportunities and sponsor engagement

04 / The Cohesive System

A connected
brand experience.

From a website concept to campaign emails, each expression brings the same approachable message into a different context.

05 / Education & Community

One mission.
Many touchpoints.

The brand experience was designed to travel seamlessly—from family education and the meal moments to partner outreach, local events, and community support.

01Website, social, and local awareness
02Food boxes, practical recipes, and resources
03Community workshops and nutrition education
04Volunteer opportunities and sponsor engagement

06 / The Content in Action

Making healthy
choices feel possible.

Practical, easy-to-follow content gives the campaign a purpose beyond visibility—helping families make informed choices with confidence.

07 / Learning Into Action

Learning happens
together.

The community nutrition workshop concept extends the campaign into a physical, hands-on experience—helping parents and children learn, connect, and build healthier routines together.

Community workshop / Concept poster

08 / Awareness & Growth

Invite. Educate.
Mobilize.

One visual identity, adapted to speak to families, volunteers, and educators while keeping the message human, welcoming, and full of energy.

09 / Partnerships & Scale

Building support
around purpose.

The sponsorship proposal adapts the brand for organizations interested in health, education, and local community partnerships. The opportunity is framed around measurable impact, shared values, and a stronger future for families.

“Good food. Quality time.
Stronger families.”
Partner engagement / Concept proposal

A mission you can
carry with you.

Proposed merchandise takes the identity beyond the digital experience into everyday life through functional products that build belonging and visibility.
